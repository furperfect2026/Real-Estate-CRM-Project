Strategic Overview

The Real Estate Sales Problem & Our Solution

Eliminating lead leakage, slow 1-by-1 manual assignment, and disconnected booking records.

⚠️ The Typical Bottlenecks
  • Manual One-by-One Assignment: Admin exhausts hours assigning individual leads to managers and callers.
  • Lead Leakage & Duplicate Waste: Unchecked portals flood duplicates, wasting executive call time.
  • Untracked Calls: Lack of duration timers, outcome dispositions, or call notes.
  • Lost Tour Momentum: Site visits scribbled on paper or WhatsApp with no cab pickup sync.
  • Disconnected Bookings: Won deals and token payments untracked against pipeline.
✅ Tathastu Infra CRM Solution
  • Multi-Lead Batch Allocation: Select all unassigned leads and assign in 1 click!
  • Automated Phone Deduplication: Instant alert detects repeat inquiries and spam.
  • Callen Cloud Dialer: Real-time call duration timer, audio wave, and disposition outcomes.
  • Operational Site Visits: Schedule tours, client cab pick-up landmarks, and tour completion.
  • Unit Bookings & Tokens: Flat/villa allotment, agreement values, and token tracking.
